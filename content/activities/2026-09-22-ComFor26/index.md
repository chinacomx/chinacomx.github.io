ChinaComx PI and Postdoc, Lena Henningsen and Damian Mandzunowski, will present an advanced version of their paper on high socialist lianhuanhua at the 21st annual conference of the [“Gesellschaft für Comicforschung” (Society for Comics Studies)](https://www.comicgesellschaft.de/en/category/comicgesellschaft/was-ist-die-comfor/) running under the theme ["Figures and Figurations of the Heroic in Comics“](https://www.comicgesellschaft.de/en/2026/06/01/21-comfor-jahrestagung-2026-figuren-und-figurationen-des-heroischen-im-comic-giessen/) on 30 Sept-1 Oct 2026 in Giessen.

Lena and Damian, in their talk titled __"Into the Maoverse: Reconfigurations of the Heroic in Chinese Socialist Comics"__, showcase an new version of their [shared paper](https://chinacomx.github.io/activities/2024-10-10-finissagehd/) informed by the work done at ChinaComx in the past year. 

Get the full onference program [here](https://www.comicgesellschaft.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ComFor-Konferenz-2026_Tagungsprogramm_Hotels.pdf).
