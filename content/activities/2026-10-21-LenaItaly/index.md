Our PI, [Lena Henningsen](https://chinacomx.github.io/team/lena/), will give an invited talk at [Ca’ Foscari University of Venice](https://www.unive.it/web/en/497/home) on 28 October 2026. She will then participate, with a presentation and a workshop, at the [BIBI International Research and Partnership Forum](https://www.childrensliterature-unipd.it/bibi-international-research-partnership-forum/) at [University of Padova](https://www.unipd.it/en) on 29 October 2026.

The talk in Venice is titled __From Maoverse to Luxuniverse: Adaptations of Popular Culture, Literature and Art into Chinese Lianhuanhua Comics__, with [Dayton Lekner](https://www.unive.it/web/en/19197/people/27767015?cHash=a01f3813f26aeba09d03b1c63da5f6c31601c0d01aac664e66206ca6c984ab72) as Chair. Lena will share the depiction of great people in _lianhuanhua_, how they are connected to one another, and how these connections ultimately constitute a vast, interconnected universe.

During the Forum in Padova, Lena will introduce the ChinaComx project and give a talk about __Chinese Comics as Children’s Literature: Entertainment, Education and Propaganda__. Her talk will begin at 18:15 at Palazzo del Bo, Aula Nievo.
