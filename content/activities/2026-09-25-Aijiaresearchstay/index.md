ChinaComx PhD candidate [Aijia Zhang](https://chinacomx.github.io/team/aijia/) is undertaking two research stays during autumn 2026 as part of her doctoral research on _lianhuanhua_.

From __21 September__ to __15 October__, she is hosted by the [Machine Visual Culture: Artificial Intelligence and the History of Seeing](https://www.biblhertz.it/en/machine-visual-culture) research group at the [Bibliotheca Hertziana – Max Planck Institute for Art History](https://www.biblhertz.it/en/home/) in __Rome__. There, she continues developing her project, “Beyond Empty Space: Quantifying Liubai in Chinese Comics (_Lianhuanhua_)”, which combines computational image analysis and digital humanities approaches to investigate the visual distribution of empty space in _lianhuanhua_ and its relationship to the Chinese aesthetic concept of _liubai_ (留白).

From __19 October__ to __20 November__, Aijia will be a guest of [Thomas Smits](https://www.uva.nl/en/profile/s/m/t.p.smits/t.p.smits.html), Assistant Professor of Digital History and AI, at the [University of Amsterdam](https://www.uva.nl/en). During this stay, she will work on building and expanding a multimodal dataset and database of _lianhuanhua_, integrating textual, visual, and bibliographic information from her dissertation corpus.
