ChinaComx PI [Lena Henningsen](https://chinacomx.github.io/team/lena/) presented her research on 2 October at the workshop [New Popular Literature and Art: Meanings, Debates, and Practices](https://uni-freiburg.de/sinologie-en/new-popular-literature/), held at the [Department of Chinese Studies](https://uni-freiburg.de/sinologie-en/), University of Freiburg. The workshop is jointly organized by [Jessica Imbach](https://uni-freiburg.de/sinologie-en/einrichtung-und-personen/junprof-dr-jessica-imbach/) and [Shi Bingbing](https://uni-freiburg.de/sinologie/einrichtung-und-personen/dr-bingbing-shi/). It is part of the ERC-funded [SINOFANTASY](https://uni-freiburg.de/sinologie/forschung-2/forschung/sinofantasy/) project. 

In her presentation, Lena explored how the visual and cultural legacy of early comic culture in China continues to shape contemporary brand marketing and socialist-coded consumer culture. For the review of the workshop, please read it [here](https://uni-freiburg.de/sinologie/queer-cybernetic-and-red-insights-from-the-workshop-new-popular-literature-and-art-meanings-debates-and-practices/).
