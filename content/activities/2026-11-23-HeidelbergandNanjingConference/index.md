You are cordially invited to the _1st Joint Conference of [Heidelberg University](https://www.uni-heidelberg.de/en) and [Nanjing University of the Arts](https://en.nua.edu.cn/)_, "__Chinese Cultural Heritage in Transmedia and Transcultural Circulation__" taking place from Thursday to Saturday, 26-28 November 2026, in HCTS Room 4400.02.212. We are happy to welcome colleagues from both host universities as well as from Prague, Göttingen, and Amsterdam.

[Thomas Smits](https://www.uva.nl/en/profile/t.p.smits) (University of Amsterdam) and [Aijia Zhang](https://chinacomx.github.io/team/aijia/) (Heidelberg University, ChinaComx project) will deliver a keynote titled "__Are We at a Multimodal Turn in Digital Humanities Research? Exploring, Enriching and Analysing Lianhuanhua Illustrations with Multimodal AI__" on Friday, from 9:30 to 10:30. The keynote is open to all participants.

Please read the full program below:
